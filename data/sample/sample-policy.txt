Section 1. Definitions
Pre-existing disease means any condition, ailment or injury diagnosed within 36 months before the first policy start date.
Reimbursement means the insured pays the hospital first and then claims the amount from the insurer.

Section 2. Waiting Periods
2.1 Initial waiting period. No claim is payable for any illness in the first 30 days from the first policy start date, except for accidents.
2.2 Specified illness waiting period. Treatment for cataract, hernia, kidney stones and joint replacement is not payable in the first 24 months.
2.3 Pre-existing disease waiting period. Pre-existing diseases are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.

Section 3. Room Rent
Room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day. If a higher category room is chosen, all associated medical expenses are reduced in the same proportion.

Section 4. Exclusions
4.1 Cosmetic surgery is not covered unless needed after an accident.
4.2 Treatment outside India is not covered.
4.3 Hospitalisation for less than 24 hours is not covered, except for listed day care procedures.

Section 5. Claim Procedure
5.1 Intimation. The insured must inform the insurer within 48 hours of an emergency admission, or at least 72 hours before a planned admission.
5.2 Reimbursement documents. Submit the claim form, original bills, discharge summary, investigation reports and payment receipts within 30 days of discharge.
5.3 Condonation. A delay in submitting documents may be condoned if the insured shows it was due to genuine hardship.

Section 6. Grievances
Write to the Grievance Officer at the address on your policy schedule. If the matter is not resolved, the insured may approach the Insurance Ombudsman.
